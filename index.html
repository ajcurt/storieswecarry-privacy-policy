The questions you keep meaning to ask your mom and dad.

Stories We Carry is an iPhone app that interviews your parents about their lives. It asks a question out loud, they answer out loud, and it follows up the way a good listener would. They never have to read the screen.

Ask to join the beta Now in a small beta on iPhone

Made for listening, not reading

Your parent doesn't need good eyesight, typing, or any tech skills. They listen and they talk.

Every word is spoken aloud

Each question is read in a natural voice, and your parent answers by talking, in their own words and at their own pace. Even the permission screens read themselves aloud. We tested it with VoiceOver, the screen reader built into every iPhone.

It listens, then asks the next good question

When they mention something, it asks about that. When they spell a name, it keeps their spelling and says it back once. If they say "I don't remember," it moves on kindly, without pressing. Next time, it remembers what they've already talked about.

No scheduling, no one else in the room

One answer over morning coffee or an hour on a quiet afternoon, and either counts as a whole conversation. You don't have to set up calls or be there to ask the questions. They open the app when they feel like talking.

Easy to stop

"Stop For Now" is always on the screen, and saying "I'd like to stop" works too. It ends warmly and picks up where they left off.

It starts with their name, then their life

It asks what they like to be called and the story behind their name. After that, it moves through the chapters of a life and comes back to any of them.

Their own voice, kept for the family

Most tools keep only the words. With your parent's permission, Stories We Carry also keeps the recording of every answer: their voice, their laugh, the way they say a name. If they'd rather not be recorded, they can say no, and everything else works the same.

An answer can only be recorded once, in the moment it's spoken, so we built recording first. Ways to share the recordings come next.

Recordings are encrypted, kept for the long term, and never used to train AI.

Planned: FamilySearch Memories

We're working toward sending these recordings to FamilySearch Memories, where families keep photos, documents and audio alongside their family tree. That isn't available yet.

What stays with the family

Each conversation is written down as they talk, and they can ask for a written copy by email. Along with the recordings, that's the start of a family archive in their own words.

Their stories are never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used to train AI. Read the privacy policy.

Who gets the written copy?
It's emailed to your parent, at the address on their own account. From there, they can share it however they like.
What does my parent need?
An iPhone and a quiet spot. Someone may need to help with the first setup and sign-in. After that, it's talking.
Is there a cost?
The beta is free. Pricing isn't set yet.

Why I built this

A lot of people mean to tell their life story and never start. They don't know where to begin, they edit themselves before they've said anything, and it never feels like there's time. I built Stories We Carry so the asking is taken care of, in short bursts or long sessions, without putting anyone out.

For me, it's part of turning the hearts of the children to their fathers, and the hearts of the fathers to their children.

AJ Curtis, founder

Why "Stories We Carry"

Every family carries stories: the ones our parents told us, and the ones only they can tell. We carry them with us, and one day we hand them to the people who come after us. The name is a reminder that those stories are ours to keep and to pass on, and that they're worth recording before they're lost.
