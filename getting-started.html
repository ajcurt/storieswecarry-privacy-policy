Getting Started with Stories We Carry

Thank you for trying this out — it means a lot.

What Stories We Carry is

Stories We Carry is a voice interview app. It asks questions about your life — childhood, family, career, the people and moments that mattered — and you answer out loud, like a conversation. It listens, remembers what you've told it, and gently follows up in later sessions.

Before you start

  • Find a quiet spot and hold the phone about as close as you would for a phone call.
  • Turn the volume up. The app asks its questions out loud.
  • Have your sign-in ready: the email and password we sent you.

Your first time

  1. Install the app from TestFlight. Open the invitation email on your iPhone, tap the link, and follow the steps. It installs Apple's free TestFlight app first, then Stories We Carry.
  2. Sign in with the email and password we sent you.
  3. Listen to "Before we begin." The app reads it to you out loud. It explains that an AI holds the conversation, and that your stories are never used to train AI. Tap I understand, let's begin.
  4. Choose whether to keep your voice. The app asks if it may keep recordings of your answers, so your family can one day hear your stories in your own voice. Tap Yes, keep my voice or No, just the words. Either way, everything else works the same.
  5. Allow the microphone when your iPhone asks. The app can't hear you without it.

Having a conversation

  1. Listen to the question.
  2. Tap Tap to Answer and talk for as long as you like. Short or long, both are fine.
  3. Tap Done Talking when you've finished. After a moment, it asks the next question.

Stopping, and getting a copy

  • Stop whenever you like. Tap Stop For Now, which is always on the screen, or just say "I'd like to stop." Next time, it picks up where you left off.
  • Get a written copy. When you stop, tap Email Me This Conversation and a copy goes to your own email address. The written words come from speech recognition, so a few may be misheard.

What to expect

  • It's a conversation, not a form. Talk however you normally would. There's no wrong way to answer.
  • If it gets a name wrong, just tell it. Spell the name out loud ("that's Jon, J-O-N") and it will remember the right spelling.
  • You can revisit anything. If a topic brings up more later, or you didn't say everything you wanted the first time, you can come back to it in a future session.
  • It's fine to pause, or say "I don't remember." The app won't push. Memory doesn't work on command, and that's okay.
  • Come back whenever you'd like. There's no schedule to keep.

Tell us what's not working

If something breaks, looks wrong, or trips you up in the moment, use TestFlight's own feedback tool: take a screenshot (or tap the feedback button in TestFlight) and add a line about what happened. That goes straight to us with the technical details attached, which is the fastest way to fix real bugs.

Tell us how it actually felt

After you've used Stories We Carry for about a week, we'll send you a short survey about the experience itself — not bugs, but things like whether the pacing felt right, whether it asked good questions, and whether this is something you'd want to keep doing. That reflective feedback is just as valuable as bug reports, so please don't skip it.
