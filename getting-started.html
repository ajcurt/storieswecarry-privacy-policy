Getting Started with Fireside

Thank you for trying this out — it means a lot.

What Fireside is

Fireside is a voice interview app. It asks questions about your life — childhood, family, career, the people and moments that mattered — and you answer out loud, like a conversation. It listens, remembers what you've told it, and gently follows up in later sessions.

What to expect

  • It's a conversation, not a form. Talk however you normally would. There's no wrong way to answer.
  • You can revisit anything. If a topic brings up more later, or you didn't say everything you wanted the first time, you can come back to it in a future session.
  • It's fine to pause, or say "I don't remember." The app won't push. Memory doesn't work on command, and that's okay.
  • Sessions are short by design. A few questions at a time, not a marathon. Stop whenever you want and pick up later.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and sign in with the account we set up for you.
  2. Start a session and just start talking when it asks you something.
  3. Come back whenever you'd like — there's no schedule to keep.

Tell us what's not working

If something breaks, looks wrong, or trips you up in the moment — use TestFlight's own feedback tool: take a screenshot (or tap the feedback button in TestFlight) and add a line about what happened. That goes straight to us with the technical details attached, which is the fastest way to fix real bugs.

Tell us how it actually felt

After you've used Fireside for about a week, we'll send you a short survey about the experience itself — not bugs, but things like whether the pacing felt right, whether it asked good questions, and whether this is something you'd want to keep doing. That reflective feedback is just as valuable as bug reports, so please don't skip it.
